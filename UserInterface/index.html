Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app drain my battery?

ScreamGuard is designed to be battery efficient. Our optimized algorithms ensure minimal power consumption while maintaining constant protection. In our tests, the app uses less than 5% of battery over an 8-hour period.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely! Your privacy is our priority. All audio processing happens locally on your device, and no audio recordings are stored or transmitted to our servers. Only location data is sent to your emergency contacts when an alert is triggered.

How accurate is the scream detection?

Our CNN model has been trained on a diverse dataset of over 900 audio samples and has achieved over 95% accuracy in detecting distress screams. The system is designed to minimize false positives while ensuring that genuine distress calls are detected promptly.

Can I customize my emergency contacts?

Yes, you can add multiple emergency contacts and prioritize them. You can also customize the alert message that will be sent to them. This feature allows you to ensure that the right people are notified in case of an emergency.

Will it work if my phone is in my pocket or bag?

Yes, ScreamGuard is designed to detect screams even when your phone is in a pocket or bag. Our advanced audio processing techniques filter out background noise and focus on distress sound patterns for reliable detection in various conditions.
